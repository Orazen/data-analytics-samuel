MASTER’S THESIS2025–2026R

Hierarchical Clusterwise Regression on Italian Emissions Data

A spatial modeling study of municipal CO₂, PM2.5, and PM10 measurements from 2021.

Approach

Iteratively merged 106 initial province-based groups using a regression residual-sum-of-squares criterion, then fitted cluster-specific ordinary least squares models after area normalisation.

Validation

Assigned validation and test observations to training clusters with a nearest-neighbour method. Compared K = 2–25 using validation RSS; K = 2 was selected.

↗

Evaluated held-out predictions with RSS and RMSE; created maps, elbow plots, and observed-versus-predicted figures in R.
