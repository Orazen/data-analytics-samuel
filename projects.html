THESIS 01 · MASTER’S2025–2026 · R

Hierarchical Clusterwise Regression on Italian Emissions Data

Can regional groupings improve regression when municipal pollution measurements vary across Italy?

DATA2021 municipal CO₂, PM2.5, and PM10
STARTING GROUPS106 province-based groups
SELECTED MODELK = 2 by validation RSS

Method

The thesis develops a hierarchical clusterwise regression workflow. It starts with 106 province-based groups, then iteratively merges groups using a regression residual-sum-of-squares criterion.

After area normalisation, Samuel fits cluster-specific ordinary least squares models for spatially referenced municipal pollutant data.

Validation and outputs

Validation and test observations are assigned to training clusters with a nearest-neighbour method. Candidate cluster counts K = 2–25 are compared on validation RSS, with K = 2 selected.

Held-out predictions are evaluated with RSS and RMSE. The R workflow produces maps, elbow plots, and observed-versus-predicted figures using ggplot2 and sf.

THESIS 02 · BACHELOR’S2023

Density-Based vs. Centroid-Based Clustering

A comparative study of two approaches to finding groups in real datasets.

DEGREEBSc in Data Analytics
COMPARISONDensity-based and centroid-based methods
APPLICATIONReal datasets

Thesis scope

Samuel investigated density-based and centroid-based clustering methods and their application to real datasets. This page records the thesis scope described in the supplied English and Italian CVs.

Methods in context

Density-based methods form groups from local neighbourhood structure, while centroid-based methods assign observations around representative centres. The thesis is presented as a method comparison, not as a claim that one family always performs better.

Available detail

The supplied CVs do not specify dataset names, evaluation metrics, or numerical findings for this thesis, so this summary does not invent them.

PROJECT REPORT 01R · STATISTICAL METHODS

Experimental Research Design

Choosing and interpreting tests for independent groups, paired observations, and multiple groups.

DATALung capacity and memory-score examples
METHODSWilcoxon rank-sum · paired t-test · one-way ANOVA
FORMATCoursework report

Question and approach

The report demonstrates how the test follows the study design: an independent-group rank test for smoker and non-smoker lung-capacity observations, a paired t-test for memory scores measured before and after a drug, and ANOVA for comparisons across several groups.

It includes visual checks and normality checks before testing. The examples are useful for discussing assumptions and test selection, rather than for making clinical claims.

PROJECT REPORT 02R · SIMULATION

Smoothing Simulated Functional Data

How does B-spline model complexity change the smoothness and error of noisy curves?

DATA80 simulated curves · 1,000 points each
METHODB-spline basis smoothing
TOOLSR · fda

Design

The project generates noisy sine curves on a 1,000-point grid from 0 to 2π, then fits smooth functions using B-spline bases. It compares 5, 50, and 200 knots and also searches basis sizes from 150 to 400.

The report tracks mean squared error and discusses bias and variance for noisy and noise-free curves. Because the curves are simulated, the results illustrate a method rather than describe an observed population.

What the report observes

Bias (conceptual) Variance (conceptual)
SCHEMATIC · NOT MEASURED PROJECT VALUES

The report describes lower smoothing bias and higher variance as the number of knots increases. It frames basis-size selection as a trade-off; the supplied narrative does not provide a single audited numeric optimum to publish.

PROJECT REPORT 04R · EXPLORATORY PSYCHOMETRICS

Exploring Structure in Big Five Survey Items

Exploratory factor analysis of a set of 26 Likert-rated personality-survey items.

DATAOnline survey · April–June 2019
ITEMS26 Likert-rated questions
TOOLSR · psych · corrplot

Workflow

The analysis checks item distributions and factorability, calculates KMO and Bartlett statistics, and uses PCA and parallel analysis to guide the number of dimensions. It then compares rotated principal-component and principal-axis solutions.

The public survey asks respondents to take a Big Five personality test online; the sample is self-selected rather than a probability sample.

Reported diagnostics

OVERALL KMO0.87Reported measure of sampling adequacy
PARALLEL ANALYSIS9 / 79 factors and 7 components, as reported

The project explores several plausible representations instead of confirming a fixed, validated questionnaire structure.

Interpretation note

Factor counts can depend on the selection rule, item treatment, and extraction method. The results are exploratory and should not be described as proof that the survey measures nine validated traits.
